# Multiple-driver steward cases

Mode: Operate. Extend the incumbent Production steward form; validate on Staging first. Exactly one decision per accused driver, explicitly confirmed by the owner.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One incident, several participants, unambiguous individual consequences. Do not split a pile-up into unrelated cases.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the dark themed form, native selects, readable labels, pill actions and existing spacing. Stewards use this alongside an external discussion on desktop or phone; no new visual world or assets.

STORY: Choose a race, add reporters and accused drivers, describe the incident, then record one decision and reason for each accused driver. Review all consequences before publishing atomically. Drafts preserve participants; failures preserve the input.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Existing heading and queue remain unchanged. The inline form starts with race and participant selectors, followed by the incident. Each accused driver has a clearly named decision row; rows stack on mobile. The publication action remains at the end with explicit confirmation.

FORM: Precisely scoped code-led extension; no concept seed applicable. Add/remove controls are native buttons with 44px targets. Duplicate participants are prevented; a driver may occupy both roles. Existing cases remain readable. No decorative motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
